Warm up your aim in 30 seconds.

Warmline is a free aim-training minigame built for the dead time before a match: waiting in queue, picking your agent, between games. Open it, play one round, get back to the game.

Play now – it runs in your browser

Flick mode

Quick targets that test your first-shot speed and accuracy.

Head height mode

Train crosshair placement at head level, the habit that wins duels.

Daily challenge

Everyone gets the same targets each day. Beat your own score.

Streaks and ranks

Build combos, earn XP, climb Warmline's own ranks and keep your daily streak alive.

Optional soundtrack

A generative soundtrack that builds with your streak. Off by default, one click to enable.

Crosshair and sensitivity tools

Design a crosshair and convert your sensitivity to cm/360 and eDPI.

What Warmline is not

  • It gives no advantage in a match. It is practice, not assistance. It never reads, overlays or interacts with a game in progress.
  • It does not access any game data or account. No login, no game files, no API calls to game services.
  • It uses no third-party game assets. All art, sounds and ranks are original to Warmline.

Roadmap

Warmline is being prepared as an in-game overlay app for the Overwolf platform, shown only when it is allowed: loading screens, between rounds and after matches. Future ideas include a lineup practice mode and a shared scoreboard for the daily challenge.
