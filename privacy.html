Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Summary

Warmline has no accounts, no login and no tracking. I do not collect your personal data on any server.

1. Data stored on your device

Warmline saves your progress (scores, level, XP, daily streak, crosshair and audio settings) in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device through Warmline, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

2. Data I collect

None. Warmline does not use analytics, advertising cookies, fingerprinting or any form of user tracking. I do not ask for or store your name, email address, game account or any game data.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts: the pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address). See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Overwolf (future): if Warmline is released as an Overwolf app, the Overwolf platform may show ads and handle subscriptions under its own privacy policy. This page will be updated before that happens.

4. Game data

Warmline does not access any video game, its files, its memory, its accounts or its APIs.

5. Children

Warmline is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Your rights

Since I hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on my side. If you have a question, write to me.

7. Changes

I may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version.
